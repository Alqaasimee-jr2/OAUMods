## 🎯 The Core Problem: The Freshman Trap
Coming straight out of secondary school into an unfamiliar town (Ile-Ife), freshers who miss on-campus bed space in Angola or Mozambique are prime targets for predatory housing cartels and fraudsters:
1. **The "Consultation / Inspection Fee" Scam**: Unscrupulous agents collect ₦3,000 to ₦10,000 upfront just to "show rooms", showing derelict houses and refusing to refund.
2. **Fake Landlords & Double-Renting**: Fraudsters collect annual rent for an apartment they don't own, or rent the exact same self-contain to 3 different unsuspecting students and vanish.
3. **Hidden Overcharging**: Charging freshers ₦350,000 for a room that returning seniors pay ₦180,000 for, plus inflated "agreement and commission" fees (sometimes up to 40% of rent).
4. **Dangerous or Power-Starved Zones**: Renting in remote, unpatrolled bush paths or areas with zero water/electricity infrastructure.

## 🛡️ The Freshman Anti-Dupe / Scam Prevention Rules

Every freshman must be guided by these **golden non-negotiable rules**:

1. **Rule #1: NEVER Pay Before Physical Inspection**
   * Any agent or "landlord" who asks for a commitment fee, holding deposit, or "fuel money for inspection" on WhatsApp before you step into the room is **100% a scammer**.
2. **Rule #2: The Triple-Verification Method**
   * Before transferring rent, talk to current student tenants already living in that compound. Ask them:
     * *"Who is the real landlord or appointed caretaker?"*
     * *"How is electricity divided (prepaid meter vs estimated)?"*
     * *"Does the borehole pump run reliably when power fails?"*
3. **Rule #3: The Standard Agency & Agreement Limit**
   * Standard agent fees in Ife should not exceed **10% to 15%** of rent. Never pay 30–40% extortionate agent charges.
4. **Rule #4: Insist on Signed Agreement & Stamped Receipt**
   * Ensure the receipt states the exact room number, tenure start/end date, and breakdown of rent, caution fee, and service charges.

## 🤝 Student Roommate Matching & Co-Living Board
Because a ₦300,000+ self-contain is too expensive for many freshmen:
* **The "Find a Roommate" Hub**:
  * Students can filter potential co-roommates by:
    * Faculty / Department (study habits compatibility).
    * Hall preference (Off-campus zone: Maintenance vs Gate vs Asherifa).
    * Lifestyle preferences (quiet study vs music, cooking preferences, early riser vs night owl).
  * Contact via verified university student email to keep predators out.
