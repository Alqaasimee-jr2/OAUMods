# 💡 Strategy Spec 03: Students' Union (SU) Partnership & Outreach Playbook

> **Source**: Audio Notes 3 & 6 (from Core Team)  
> **Status**: *Ideation / Strategic Planning*  
> **Goal**: Secure official endorsement and campus-wide distribution via the Great Ife Students' Union (SUG)

---

## 🎯 The Core Philosophy: "Build First, Pitch Second"
As rightly observed in the team audio notes:
> *"We're going to start small-small, sure now... there's a way we can present it to SU people, that is after the site must be functioning well."*

Pitching an unbuilt "idea" to student union politics invites delays and bureaucracy. But presenting a **live, functioning, high-aesthetic web application** that is already solving freshers' problems makes partnership an easy win.

---

## 📅 The 3-Stage Outreach Timeline

### Stage 1: Private Alpha Validation (Where We Are Now)
*   Finalize the ideation framework.
*   Validate the prototype within our room, dev group, and trusted inner circle.
*   Ensure zero bugs, fast loading speeds, and verified campus data.

### Stage 2: Direct 1-on-1 Executive Outreach
*   Rather than going through a formal committee, leverage personal relationships:
    1.  **SU Welfare Director**: The most important student official for freshmen. Their primary mandate is student hostel welfare, clearance complaints, and orientation.
    2.  **SU Public Relations Officer (PRO)**: The official broadcaster of union releases. They can broadcast the link directly to thousands of freshers.
    3.  **SU President / Vice President**: For high-level endorsement.
*   **The Pitch Message**:
    > *"We built an open-source, 100% non-profit campus companion for Great Ife freshers called OAUMods. It has verified clearance checklists, hostel guides, transit fares, and campus navigation. It costs the union ₦0, has no ads, and solves the repetitive questions your welfare desk answers daily. We would love the Union's official blessing to make this the default guide for this year's orientation."*

### Stage 3: Official Union Integration & Seeding
Once the Union reviews the working platform:
*   **Official WhatsApp & Telegram Seeding**: Link pinned across official union groups.
*   **Physical Touchpoints**: QR codes placed on SUG notice boards at the Ken Saro-Wiwa building (SUB), Angola, and Mozambique.
*   **Orientation Week Integration**: Demoed during the official university-wide Freshman Orientation ceremony at the Amphitheatre.
