# 💡 Feature Spec 02: Hostel-to-Lecture Hall Navigation (Angola & Moz ➔ Campus)

> **Source**: Audio Notes 2 & 5 (from Core Team)  
> **Status**: *Ideation / Review*  
> **Target Audience**: All 100-Level Residents in Angola Hall & Mozambique Hall

---

## 🎯 The Core Problem
OAU has a sprawling 11,000+ hectare estate with iconic architectural complexes connected by long paved walkways, dirt shortcuts, and road networks. 
* 100% of undergraduate freshmen live in **Angola Hall** (males) and **Mozambique Hall** (females).
* On Day 1 of classes, thousands of freshers panic trying to figure out:
  * *"How do I get to BOOC for CHM101 or BIO101 before 8:00 AM?"*
  * *"Is it faster to walk or take a Keke from Moz to White House?"*
  * *"Where is the secret shortcut to Spider House?"*

---

## 🗺️ Key Freshman Corridors & Visual Route Cards

### Route 1: Angola / Moz ➔ BOOC (Biological Sciences Lecture Theatre)
*   **Target Courses**: CHM101, BIO101, General Science & Health Sciences classes.
*   **Walking Time**: 8–12 Minutes.
*   **Recommended Method**: Walking (Keke is unnecessary).
*   **Step-by-Step Waypoint Guidance**:
    1.  Exit Angola / Moz main gate toward the central hostel avenue.
    2.  Pass the University Health Centre ("JAC") on your right (opposite Alumni Hall).
    3.  Take the paved pedestrian walkway heading directly toward the Science complex.
    4.  BOOC is the massive tiered auditorium building directly ahead with wide front stairways.

### Route 2: Angola / Moz ➔ White House (Faculty of Science)
*   **Target Departments**: Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, Geology.
*   **Walking Time**: 12–15 Minutes (or 4 mins Keke to SUB + 3 mins walk).
*   **Recommended Method**: Walking through the central walkway past Moremi Hall.
*   **Step-by-Step Waypoint Guidance**:
    1.  Head past the Moz gate toward the central axis.
    2.  Follow the walkway leading toward Moremi Hall and the Motion Ground lawn.
    3.  Cross past the SUB parking area; White House is the distinctive white-pillared science quadrangle.

### Route 3: Angola / Moz ➔ Spider House (Faculty of Technology)
*   **Target Departments**: Computer Science, Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Chemical Engineering.
*   **Walking Time**: 14–18 Minutes.
*   **Recommended Method**: Walk via the Civil Engineering back-path or take a Keke to Maintenance Gate and enter the Tech zone.
*   **Visual Cue**: Look for the open steel roof trusses that resemble spider legs.

### Route 4: Angola / Moz ➔ ODLT 1 & 2 / AUD
*   **Target Departments**: Social Sciences, Arts, Humanities, Economics, Law.
*   **Walking Time**: 12–15 Minutes across Motion Ground.
*   **Visual Cue**: Adjacent to the Amphitheatre and Oduduwa Hall.

---

## 📱 Proposed UI Presentation
Instead of requiring high-bandwidth live GPS (which often lags on campus 3G):
1.  **Curated "Swipeable Route Cards"**: Visual step-by-step waypoint cards with photos of landmarks along the path.
2.  **Time & Fare Comparison**: Shows *Walking Time (Free)* vs *Keke Route (Fare + Drop-off)*.
3.  **Shortcut Warnings**: Indicate which dirt paths get muddy during the rainy season.
