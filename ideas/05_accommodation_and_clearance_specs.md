## 🏠 1. Accommodation & Bed Space Engine

### A. The Bed Space Balloting Reality at OAU
*   **The Problem**: Over 8,000 freshmen compete for ~2,500 spaces across Angola (males) and Mozambique (females). Balloting opens at an announced hour (typically 10:00 AM) and is exhausted in minutes.
*   **The 72-Hour Window**: Allocations expire automatically if the student does not pay the bank fee within 72 hours.
*   **Anti-Scam Defense**: Severe warning against touts claiming to sell spaces; bed spaces are hardcoded to personal matriculation numbers.

### B. Off-Campus Student Residential Zones
When on-campus bed space is missed, students need reliable pricing and safety intelligence:
1.  **Maintenance Axis**: Top choice for freshers; 5-10 mins walking distance to Angola/Moz; high electricity uptime; ₦180k–₦350k.
2.  **Campus Gate / AP Axis**: Fast transit via shuttle buses; abundant food spots and banks; ₦150k–₦280k.
3.  **Mayfair / Lagere Axis**: Town center; lowest grocery prices; requires daily ₦200–₦300 transit; ₦120k–₦250k.
4.  **Asherifa / Parakin Axis**: Modern self-contained studio flats; high security; ₦200k–₦450k.
