## 📅 2026-09-18 — Entry 001: The Spark, The Vision & Setting The Compass

### 💭 Context & The Spark
*   Today marks the official genesis of the project. The journey began with a clear realization: arriving at Obafemi Awolowo University (OAU / Great Ife) as a freshman is unnecessarily stressful. From bed space balloting in Angola and Mozambique to chaotic physical clearance across faculties, new students navigate blind.
*   We committed to building a dedicated, open-source, non-profit software companion tailored specifically to this micro-macro niche.
*   The philosophy: **Build from what works.** We researched proven global and student-led precedents (NUSMods, Guidebook Higher Ed, TUM Campus App) to identify the core pillars that turn a campus guide into an indispensable survival tool.

### 🧠 Strategic Debates & Key Decisions
1.  **Solo vs. Stranger Recruitment Debate**:
    *   *Question*: Should we recruit strangers from campus groups immediately or build solo?
    *   *Decision*: **Core Team First, Public Call with Working Prototype.** Recruiting strangers on an "idea" invites bikeshedding, tech stack disputes, and high attrition. Instead, the project lead + assistant + roommates build the working v0 prototype first. When a live working link exists, momentum will attract top campus contributors.
2.  **Roommate Activation**:
    *   The project lead sent an intimate broadcast message to his roommates to bring them in as the first sounding board, early testers, and data contributors.
3.  **The Three Pillars of Project Governance**:
    *   Decided to anchor the project in three living Markdown files before writing any application code:
        *   `README.md`: The project identity, soul, and manifesto.
        *   `MILESTONES.md`: An in-depth, realistic milestone tracker to guide the journey.
        *   `CHANGELOG.md`: This comprehensive project diary logging every interaction, thought, and change.
4.  **Platform Choice**:
    *   Committed to a **Mobile Web / Progressive Web App (PWA)** approach. Fast, zero-install friction, instant updates, and 100% offline caching for spotty campus network zones.

### 🎯 Milestone Status & Immediate Tasks
*   [x] **Phase 0.1**: Market research and deconstruction of successful live examples (NUSMods, Guidebook, TUM Campus).
*   [x] **Phase 0.2**: Formulation of project identity and mission.
*   [x] **Phase 0.3**: Implementation of Living Documentation System (`README.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`, `MILESTONES.md`, `PROJECT_RULES.md`).
*   [ ] **Phase 0.4**: Scaffolding the frontend architecture and base design system.

### 👥 People & Community
*   **Project Lead**: Set the overarching vision, directed focus onto OAU campus pain points, and initiated roommate collaboration.
*   **Roommates**: Broadcast message sent. Awaiting their early thoughts, clearance war stories, and specific departmental pain points.

### ⏭️ Next Immediate Step

## 📅 2026-09-18 — Entry 002: Name Locked ("OAUMods") & Landscape Verification

### 💭 Context & Thoughts
*   Before committing to the build, the Project Lead asked for an exhaustive web and app store search to ensure we are not duplicating an existing project at Obafemi Awolowo University.
*   The results confirmed a massive strategic greenlight: there is **no existing campus guide or freshman companion app** active for OAU. The campus relies solely on unguided ePortal transactions and messy WhatsApp groups.

### 🧠 Strategic Debates & Key Decisions
1.  **Brand Selection**:
    *   Evaluated naming styles inspired by NUSMods (NUS + Mods) and TUM Campus.
    *   The Project Lead officially locked in the name: **OAUMods**.
    *   *Why this wins*: It pays homage to the legendary student-engineered NUSMods, sounds developer-native, and conveys a modular, ever-evolving toolkit for Great Ife students.
2.  **OAU Institutional Reality Confirmed**:
    *   Web intelligence verified that OAU does **not** charge an "Acceptance Fee" (unlike other federal institutions). This was immediately flagged as a high-value scam alert for freshmen to be built into the survival guide.
    *   Halls of residence for freshers: Angola (Males) and Mozambique (Females).

### 🎯 Milestone Status & Immediate Tasks
*   [x] Competitive landscape & feasibility audit completed.
*   [x] Official brand identity established: **OAUMods**.
*   [ ] **Phase 0.4**: Scaffold the mobile-first frontend web app with offline capabilities.

### 👥 People & Community
*   **Project Lead**: Selected the official name **OAUMods** and gave the green light for technical scaffolding.

### ⏭️ Next Immediate Step

## 📅 2026-09-18 — Entry 003: The Core Trio Assembles (Project Lead, Tobi, Richard)

### 💭 Context & Thoughts
*   The project transitioned from a solo concept into an active team effort. The Project Lead formed the dedicated dev group bringing in **Tobi** and **Richard** as co-founding core team members.
*   The philosophy set for the team: *High energy, fun, zero corporate stiffness, but focused on building something that will genuinely impact the Great Ife campus.*

### 🧠 Strategic Debates & Key Decisions
1.  **The "Plus-One Trust" Scaling Mechanism**:
    *   Instead of opening the floodgates to a public group where quality and accountability degrade, the team instituted the **Plus-One Rule**: each core member invites exactly **one** trusted, high-signal person (developer, designer, or campus data curator).
    *   This caps the initial team at 6 sharp, accountable people who can collaborate without bikeshedding.
2.  **Product Execution Strategy**:
    *   While the team introduces themselves and brings their +1s, the Project Lead and AI pair programmer will immediately stand up the **v0 working interactive prototype** (Clearance Tracker + OAU Campus Atlas) so the team rallies around a live, clickable product.
