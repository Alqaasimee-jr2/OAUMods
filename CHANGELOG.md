## 📅 2026-09-18 — Entry 001: The Spark, The Vision & Setting The Compass

### 💭 Context & The Spark
*   Today marks the official genesis of the project. The journey began with a clear realization: arriving at Obafemi Awolowo University (OAU / Great Ife) as a freshman is unnecessarily stressful. From bed space balloting in Angola and Mozambique to chaotic physical clearance across faculties, new students navigate blind.
*   We committed to building a dedicated, open-source, non-profit software companion tailored specifically to this micro-macro niche.
*   The philosophy: **Build from what works.** We researched proven global and student-led precedents (NUSMods, Guidebook Higher Ed, TUM Campus App) to identify the core pillars that turn a campus guide into an indispensable survival tool.

### 🧠 Strategic Debates & Key Decisions
1.  **Solo vs. Stranger Recruitment Debate**:
    *   *Question*: Should we recruit strangers from campus groups immediately or build solo?
    *   *Decision*: **Core Team First, Public Call with Working Prototype.** Recruiting strangers on an "idea" invites bikeshedding, tech stack disputes, and high attrition. Instead, the project lead + assistant + roommates build the working v0 prototype first. When a live working link exists, momentum will attract top campus contributors.
2.  **Roommate Activation**:
    *   The project lead sent an intimate broadcast message to his roommates to bring them in as the first sounding board, early testers, and data contributors.
3.  **The Three Pillars of Project Governance**:
    *   Decided to anchor the project in three living Markdown files before writing any application code:
        *   `README.md`: The project identity, soul, and manifesto.
        *   `MILESTONES.md`: An in-depth, realistic milestone tracker to guide the journey.
        *   `CHANGELOG.md`: This comprehensive project diary logging every interaction, thought, and change.
4.  **Platform Choice**:
    *   Committed to a **Mobile Web / Progressive Web App (PWA)** approach. Fast, zero-install friction, instant updates, and 100% offline caching for spotty campus network zones.

### 🎯 Milestone Status & Immediate Tasks
*   [x] **Phase 0.1**: Market research and deconstruction of successful live examples (NUSMods, Guidebook, TUM Campus).
