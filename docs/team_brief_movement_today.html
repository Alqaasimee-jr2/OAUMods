Core Operating Philosophy

"We're building the skeleton and the muscles holding the skeleton together first. Advanced additions roll out progressively as we grow."

📌 Strategic Alignment on Team Feedback (NUSMods & TUM)

The team member's voice notes brought high-value analysis modeled after NUSMods (open-source timetable & map routing) and TUM Munich (all-in-one student portal). While we embrace this long-term trajectory, attempting full campus-wide timetabling and live GPS tracking immediately creates massive data maintenance drag. Our agreement is clear: anchor the basics today, then scale features progressively.

⚡ Our 3-Step Movement for Today

Movement 1 Architecture & Build
Build Layer A (Public Info Portal) & Layer B (OAUMods Companion)

Construct the foundational two-layer structure that forms the core backbone of the project:

  • Layer A (Public Orientation & Info Portal): High-impact hero introducing Great Ife culture, freshman halls (Angola for males, Mozambique for females, plus Fajuyi & Awolowo), landmark directory (Oduduwa Hall, SUB, Motion Ground, Pit Theatre), and campus lingo.
  • Layer B (OAUMods Companion App): Seamlessly accessible freshman utility suite featuring the Freshman Clearance Checklist (persisted via localStorage), 100-Level Course Helper, and Venue Directory (BOO, 1k LT, AUD).
Movement 2 Multi-Device QA
Rigorous Interaction, Viewport & Offline Testing

Stress-test the unified skeleton across real-world campus constraints before external integrations:

  • Viewport Integrity: Strict validation across 360px, 390px, and 414px viewports to eliminate horizontal scrolling leaks and ensure minimum 48px touch targets.
  • Offline-First Resilience: Verify that the app and its stored checklist/course data load and run smoothly without active internet connectivity.
  • State Retention: Guarantee that freshman clearance checklist marks and custom courses survive page refreshes and browser restarts.
Movement 3 Visual & UX Polish
Extreme UI/UX Elevation (No Vibe Code / No Agent Slop)

Elevate the product aesthetic beyond a "plain school website" to a world-class consumer-grade app:

  • Curated OAU Palette: Deep OAU Navy (#0A192F), Rich Ochre Gold (#D97706), and Forest Emerald (#059669).
  • Modern Design Standards: Refined frosted glassmorphism, responsive micro-animations (<150ms transitions), and crystal-clear typographic hierarchy.
  • Mobile Dock Navigation: Smooth, thumb-friendly bottom app dock designed for seamless navigation while walking between lecture halls.

🔭 What Comes Next (Progressive Phase 2 & Beyond)

Once today's skeleton and muscles are rock-solid and verified, we will progressively introduce: (1) Leaflet.js + OpenStreetMap integration with walking time prompts from Angola/Moz, (2) Supabase user accounts & notes, and (3) Faculty-wide timetable builder with manual slot adjustments.
