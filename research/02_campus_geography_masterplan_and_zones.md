## 1. Land Holdings & The Master Plan Concept

### The 13,000-Acre Land Holding
* **Land Area**: The university occupies an immense estate comprising **13,000 acres** (approximately **11,861 hectares**) granted by the ancient kingdom of Ile-Ife under the reign of the Ooni of Ife, Oba Adesoji Aderemi.
* **Topography**: Set amidst undulating tropical hills, granite outcrops, lush rainforest valleys, and seasonal stream beds.
* **The Sharon "Three-Community" Master Plan**:
  Arieh Sharon’s master plan segregated the vast land grant into three distinct, interdependent functional communities:
  1. **The High-Density Central Academic Core**: A compact, pedestrian-first urban quadrangle housing administrative governance, lecture theatres, faculties, and central libraries connected by shaded walkways.
  2. **The Student Residential Quadrangles**: Self-contained hostel villages clustered within walking distance of the academic core, organized by gender, seniority, and social life.
  3. **The Dispersed Staff Residential Community**: Generously spaced single-family residential villas and duplexes nestled into the surrounding hills, ensuring tranquility, privacy, and faculty quality of life.

## 2. Arterial Road Networks & Transit Spines

The university is navigated via a numbered arterial road grid:

### Road 1 (Oba Adesoji Aderemi Road)
* **Status**: The primary arterial spine and main entrance ceremonial highway of the university.
* **Span**: Runs from the **Campus Main Gate** (along the Ife-Ibadan Expressway) directly into the central academic concourse.
* **Key Landmarks En Route**: Campus Security Gate Post, University Lake, Agricultural Greenhouses, Sports Complex, Senate Building, Bank Road Junction, and the Motion Ground roundabout.

### Road 2
* **Status**: Secondary arterial road linking the central core to the science and technology faculties, engineering drawing blocks, and laboratory complexes.

### Road 7
* **Status**: The residential and faculty arterial corridor.
* **Span**: Connects the central campus to the Senior Staff Residential Quarters, Professor Villas, Staff Club, and the Vice-Chancellor’s Lodge.

### Maintenance Road (Staff Quarters Axis)
* **Status**: A crucial logistical route running behind Angola and Mozambique Halls.
* **Significance for Freshmen**: Houses the **Maintenance Gate**—the primary pedestrian and Keke shortcut connecting campus hostels to popular private off-campus accommodations (Staff Quarters Road, Maintenance axis).

## 3. The 11 Functional Campus Zones

### Zone 1: The Central Academic Concourse
* **Core Buildings**: Senate Building, Hezekiah Oluwasanmi Library, Oduduwa Hall, The Secretariat.
* **Landmark Feature**: **Motion Ground**—the expansive grass lawn that serves as the visual and physical anchor of the university.

### Zone 2: Science Quadrangle
* **Core Buildings**: White House (Faculty of Science), Biological Sciences Complex (BOOC, BOOA, BOOB), Chemistry Laboratories, Physics Block, Geology Museum.
* **Student Reality**: The highest pedestrian foot traffic during morning 8:00 AM lectures.

### Zone 3: Technology & Engineering Zone
* **Core Buildings**: Spider House, Civil Engineering Workshop, Mechanical/Civil Wing, Chemical Engineering LT A & B, Electrical Engineering Labs, Computer Science Center.
* **Atmosphere**: Heavy engineering machinery, tech hubs, and technical drawing corridors.

### Zone 4: Humanities & Arts Quadrangle
* **Core Buildings**: Humanities Blocks I, II & III (Inverted Pyramids), AUD 1 & 2, Humanities 1000-Seater Lecture Theatre, Department of Dramatic Arts, Pit Theatre.

### Zone 5: Social Sciences & Administration Complex
* **Core Buildings**: Faculty of Social Sciences Building (Yellow-Panel Complex), Faculty of Administration Complex, First Bank LT, Admin (1st Bank) Lecture Theatre.

### Zone 6: Medical Sciences, Pharmacy & Health Services Precincts
* **Academic Wing (Road 2 Academic Belt)**: **College of Health Sciences (CHS)** complex (Basic Medical Sciences, Anatomy, Physiology, Medical Biochemistry, Dentistry, and Clinical training complexes), **Faculty of Pharmacy** auditoriums, and Drug Research & Production Unit (DRPU).
* **Clinical Primary Care Wing (Residential Hostel Precinct)**: **University Health Centre ("JAC")** — situated opposite Alumni Hall and beside Awolowo Hall, providing 24/7 outpatient/inpatient healthcare, diagnostic lab tests, chest X-rays, and prescription pharmacy for students and staff.
* **Spatial Reality Check**: The College of Health Sciences (academic instruction on Road 2) and the University Health Centre (primary clinical care in the student residential area) are in completely different parts of campus. Shuttles to OAUTHC load at CHS on Road 2, never at the Health Centre.

### Zone 7: Student Residential Area 1 (Freshmen Enclave)
* **Halls**: **Angola Hall** (Male Freshmen) & **Mozambique Hall** (Female Freshmen).
* **Characteristics**: Self-contained freshman precinct with 24/7 porter surveillance, convenience butteries, and immediate access to the Health Center and Maintenance Gate.

### Zone 8: Student Residential Area 2 (Returning Students & Activism)
* **Halls**: **Awolowo Hall (Awo)**, **Fajuyi Hall (Faj)**, and **ETF Hall**.
* **Characteristics**: Buzzing with round-the-clock political debates, Aroism, laundry kiosks, New Market eateries, and the Fajuyi bus stop.

### Zone 9: Central Residential & Academic Border
* **Halls**: **Moremi Hall** (Female returning undergraduates) and **Alumni Hall**.
* **Advantage**: Under 3 minutes walking distance to Hezekiah Library and Motion Ground.

### Zone 10: Commercial, Banking & Transport Terminal
* **Hubs**: Student Union Building (SUB), Bank Road (Commercial ATM Gallery), SUB Bus Terminal, New Market.
* **Services**: Retail food outlets, bookshops, cybercafés, bank branches, and student union offices.

### Zone 11: Agricultural Farm & Research Basin
* **Institutes**: Teaching and Research Farm (TRF), Centre for Energy Research and Development (CERD), Regional Centre for Training in Aerospace Surveys (RECTAS), African Regional Centre for Space Science and Technology Education (ARCSSTE-E).
* **Land Area**: Thousands of expansive hectares dedicated to crop trials, livestock husbandry, and nuclear/space science research.

## 4. Campus Gates & External Boundaries

1. **Campus Main Gate (The Toll Gate Axis)**:
   - Situated on the Ife-Ibadan Expressway.
   - The primary gateway for travelers from Ibadan, Lagos, Osogbo, and Akure.
   - Major transit hub for campus shuttle buses into the interior.
2. **Maintenance Walking Gate**:
   - Small, secure pedestrian and motorcycle gate behind Angola Hall.
   - Critical transit corridor for students living off-campus in the Maintenance/Staff Quarters zone.
3. **Parakin / Asherifa Border**:
   - Northern boundary connecting campus perimeter roads to high-end student residential estates in Parakin and Asherifa.
