## 2. Student Support & Welfare Infrastructure

### A. Division of Student Affairs (DSA)
* **Location**: Senate Building Annex & SUB Office Suite.
* **Leadership**: Dean of Student Affairs & Vice-Dean of Student Affairs.
* **Mandate**:
  - Direct oversight of on-campus hostel allocations and Hall Wardens.
  - Certification of student clubs, departmental associations, and campus fellowships.
  - Administration of scholarships, financial aid, and work-study programs.
  - Enforcement of student code of conduct and disciplinary hearings.
* **Guidance and Counselling Unit**: Located within DSA; provides free, confidential psychological support, mental health counseling, and academic stress therapy.

### B. Directorate of Medical Services (University Health Center / JAC)
* **Location**: Residential / Hostel Precinct (opposite Alumni Hall, beside Awolowo Hall; easily accessible from Angola and Mozambique Halls).
* **Role**: Primary healthcare provider for the entire university community.
* **The "Green Card" Privileges**:
  - Every registered student receives a laminated **Health Center Patient Card ("Green Card")**.
  - Grants **100% free medical consultations, standard laboratory investigations, and essential prescription drugs** throughout their undergraduate degree.
* **Emergency Dispatch**: Operates a 24/7 emergency response unit with on-call doctors, nurses, and ambulance dispatch (`+234 803 392 4118`).
* **JAC vs. OAUTHC**:
  - *University Health Center (JAC)*: On-campus outpatient and short-stay clinic for common student ailments (malaria, infections, minor trauma).
  - *OAUTHC (Teaching Hospital)*: Tertiary hospital located off-campus along Ilesa Road for complex surgeries, intensive care, and specialized medical referrals.

### C. Campus Security Unit
* **Mandate**: Campus policing, surveillance, traffic regulation, and crime prevention.
* **Key Posts**: Main Gate Command Post, SUB Satellite Post, and roving motorized patrols.
* **Emergency Contact**: `+234 803 721 9540` *(Main Gate Command)* / `+234 802 884 1221` *(SUB Post)*.

## 3. The Academic Calendar: Harmattan & Rain Semesters

Unlike universities using generic "First / Second Semester" labels, Great Ife operates under the authentic, seasonally grounded nomenclature:

```
                            [ THE OAU ACADEMIC SESSION ]
                                         │
                 ┌───────────────────────┴───────────────────────┐
                 ▼                                               ▼
       [ HARMATTAN SEMESTER ]                           [ RAIN SEMESTER ]
       • Equivalent to 1st Semester                     • Equivalent to 2nd Semester
       • Typically: October ➔ March                     • Typically: March/April ➔ July/August
       • Orientation, Matriculation,                    • Students' Week, Final Exams,
         Mid-Semester Tests, Final Exams                  Graduation Clearance
```

### Academic Milestones Timeline
1. **Resumption & Course Registration**: The opening two weeks of each semester.
2. **The 2-Week Add/Drop Window**: Strictly enforced window allowing students to add or drop courses without penalty.
3. **Mid-Semester Continuous Assessment (CA)**: Continuous assessments, quizzes, and practical tests contributing 30% to 40% of the total semester grade.
4. **Matriculation (Harmattan Semester)**: Formal swearing-in of freshmen.
5. **Revision Week ("Reading Week")**: One week preceding final exams designated for uninterrupted study; lectures are prohibited.
6. **Final Semester Examinations**: 2–3 weeks of comprehensive examinations.

## 4. Core Academic Regulations & Survival Policies

### The Credit Unit Load Thresholds
* **Standard Semester Range**: **15 to 24 Credit Units**.
* **Freshman Recommendation**: **18 to 21 Credit Units**.
* **Minimum Permissible Load**: 15 units (registering below 15 units requires formal Senate waiver).
* **Maximum Permissible Load**: 24 units (registering above 24 units requires special approval from the Faculty Board of Studies).

### The Strict 48-Hour Exam Illness Rule
* **Regulation**: If a student is prevented from sitting an official semester examination due to sudden acute illness or hospitalization, they **MUST report to the University Health Center (JAC) within 48 hours**.
* **Penalty**: If the medical condition is not certified by a designated OAU Medical Officer within 48 hours of the scheduled exam, the student receives an automatic **'F' grade (0.0 Quality Points)**.
* **Consequence**: An uncertified missed exam cannot be retaken as a special exam and must be registered as a carryover course in the subsequent session.

### Academic Probation & Withdrawal Policy
* **Grade Point Average (GPA)**: Computed on a 5.0 scale at the conclusion of each semester.
* **Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA)**: Weighted cumulative performance across all semesters.
* **Probation Threshold**: Any student whose CGPA falls below **1.00** at the end of an academic session is placed on **Academic Probation**.
* **Withdrawal ("Advice to Withdraw")**: A student on academic probation who fails to raise their CGPA to 1.00 or higher in the subsequent session is formally advised to withdraw from the university.
