## 1. The Great Ife Students' Union (SUG) Governance Structure

The Students' Union at Obafemi Awolowo University operates under a tripartite constitutional structure mirroring a federal democratic system:

```
                      [ THE GREAT IFE STUDENTS' UNION ]
                                      │
         ┌────────────────────────────┼────────────────────────────┐
         ▼                            ▼                            ▼
[ THE CENTRAL EXECUTIVE ]    [ THE STUDENTS' REP ]        [ THE JUDICIAL COUNCIL ]
     COUNCIL (CEC)              COUNCIL (SRC)             (Arbitration, Constitutional
(President, PRO, Welfare)      (Parliamentary Body)            Interpretation)
         │
         ▼
[ HALL EXECUTIVES ] & [ FACULTY / DEPARTMENTAL PRESIDENTS ]
```

### A. The Central Executive Council (CEC)
The administrative engine of student representation:
1. **The President**: Chief spokesperson, coordinates union advocacy, and chairs executive council meetings.
2. **The Vice-President**: Focuses on academic welfare, tutorial schemes, and fresher orientation coordination.
3. **The Secretary-General & Assistant Secretary-General**: Custodians of union correspondence, meeting minutes, and official records.
4. **The Public Relations Officer (PRO)**:
   - **Critical Role for Freshmen**: The PRO is the official voice of the union.
   - Disseminates authentic broadcast notices on exam postponements, tuition deadlines, transport adjustments, and union congresses.
5. **The Welfare Officer**:
   - Manages price regulation for campus transport (shuttles, kekes) and food cafeterias.
   - Directly intervenes in cases of student victimization, hostel water shortages, and health emergencies.
6. **The Financial Secretary & Treasurer**: Oversees union budgeting and financial audit trails.
7. **The Director of Socials**: Coordinates campus entertainment, cultural shows, and Fresher's Welcome concerts.
8. **The Director of Sports**: Organizes inter-faculty games, the Vice-Chancellor's Cup, and sports complex tournaments.

### B. The Students' Representative Council (SRC)
* **Status**: The legislative and parliamentary chamber of the union.
* **Composition**: Elected student parliamentarians ("Honourables") representing distinct faculties and residential halls.
* **Meetings**: Held at the SUB Parliamentary Chamber or designated lecture theatres, characterized by rigorous ideological debates and scrutiny of executive proposals.

### C. The Supreme Authority: The Student Congress
* In Great Ife jurisprudence, **The Congress** (the physical assembly of all registered students) is the highest decision-making authority, with powers exceeding both the CEC and SRC.
* Congresses are convened at the **Amphitheatre**, **Motion Ground**, or **Awolowo Hall Cafe Quadrangle** to deliberate on major campus crises, fee hikes, and national student actions.

## 2. Campus Press & Media Architecture

Great Ife has one of the most intellectually fierce and independent campus press systems in West Africa:

### The Association of Campus Journalists (ACJ OAU)
* Founded to uphold investigative reporting, freedom of expression, and ethical journalism across campus.
* Coordinates dozens of faculty, departmental, and residential press organizations.

### Iconic Hall & Faculty Press Boards
* **Awo Press**: Known for sharp political commentary, editorial cartoons, and hard-hitting administrative critique.
* **Faj Press & Moz Press**: Focusing on hostel welfare, social events, and student life.
* **Online Platforms & Student Portals**: *OAU Peeps News Agency*, departmental news blogs, and student broadcast networks.

## 3. The Daily Campus Economy & Student Sustenance

### Food Spots, Cafeterias & Late-Night Sustenance
1. **Awo Cafe (Awolowo Hall)**:
   - Legendary among Great Ife students for providing the largest, most affordable late-night servings of jollof rice, beans, plantain, and beef.
   - Operating hours: 6:00 PM – 2:00 AM (the prime hub during night reading sessions).
2. **Fajuyi Buttery Strip**:
   - The primary breakfast and quick-bite corridor.
   - Serves hot bread and fried eggs, instant noodles, meat pies, shawarma, and fresh juices.
3. **SUB Cafeteria**:
   - The central indoor dining facility.
   - Specializes in hot traditional Yoruba swallows: *Amala dudu, Semo, and Pounded Yam* paired with *Gbegiri, Ewedu, and Ogunfe (goat meat)*.
4. **Mozambique Hall Inner Food Stalls**:
   - Offers packaged rice, fried fish, fruit salads, pastries, and snacks tailored to female freshmen.
5. **New Market (Behind Fajuyi Hall)**:
   - A full-scale traditional market on campus grounds.
   - The go-to location for cooking students to purchase raw foodstuffs: tubers of yam, garri, rice, beans, palm oil, fresh tomatoes, peppers, and fish at wholesale market prices.

### Financial Infrastructure & Cash Flow
* **Bank Road**: Located between Road 1 and the SUB concourse. Houses full-service commercial banking branches and ATM galleries for **First Bank, Wema Bank, Zenith Bank, Access Bank, GTBank, and UBA**.
* **The Morning ATM Hack**: Students are advised to withdraw cash before **8:30 AM** or after **8:00 PM** to avoid the mid-day heat and 45-minute queues.
* **Point-of-Sale (POS) Network**: Ubiquitous POS agents operate inside all hall butteries and outside the library for emergency cash withdrawals.

* **Cellular Data Zones**:
