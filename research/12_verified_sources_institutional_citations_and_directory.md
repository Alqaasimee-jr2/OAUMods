## 🛡️ 6. Verification Protocol for Future Contributors

When contributing data to OAUMods, all campus data collectors and open-source contributors must adhere to the following **Verification Standard**:
1.  **Rule of Two Independent Confirmations**: No landmark room number, fee rate, or administrative rule may be codified without confirmation from at least two distinct sources (e.g. physical confirmation at the office + official published circular).
2.  **No Unverified Social Rumors**: WhatsApp broadcast rumors regarding dress code bans, purported exam date changes, or fee waivers must never be committed without official authentication from the University PRO or the respective Faculty Officer.
3.  **Date-Stamping**: Every fee or policy change must be stamped with the relevant academic session (e.g., *2023/2024 Session*).
