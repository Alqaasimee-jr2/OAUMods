### Detailed Hall Profiles

#### 1. Angola Hall (Male Freshmen Enclave)
* **Designation**: Exclusively for 100-Level Male Freshmen.
* **Location**: Hostel Area 1, adjacent to Health Sciences Road and Maintenance Gate.
* **Capacity**: Approx. 1,500+ freshmen.
* **Cultural Life**: Intense bonding, evening quadrangle football tournaments, debate circles, and early introduction to campus life.
* **Security & Curfew**: The main gate closes strictly at **10:00 PM**. Porter’s lodge operates 24/7 on the Ground Floor of Block 1.
* **Survival Pro-Tip**: Angola is a 10-minute walk to BOOC via the Health Sciences path; taking a Keke is unnecessary.

#### 2. Mozambique Hall ("Moz" - Female Freshmen Enclave)
* **Designation**: Exclusively for 100-Level Female Freshmen.
* **Location**: Hostel Area 1, across from Angola Hall.
* **Capacity**: Approx. 1,800+ freshmen.
* **Internal Ecosystem**: Features an active self-contained commercial quadrangle with hair salons, grocery shops, tailor stalls, laundry depots, and hot food vendors.
* **Security & Curfew**: Enforces a **strict 10:00 PM curfew**. Male visitors are strictly prohibited beyond the outer reception pavilion.
* **Survival Pro-Tip**: Collect water early in the morning between 6:00 AM and 7:00 AM before pumping cycles end.

#### 3. Awolowo Hall ("Awo Hall" - Male Returning Undergraduates)
* **Designation**: Male Returning Undergraduates.
* **Reputation**: The **"Political Headquarters of Great Ife"** and the birthplace of **Aroism**.
* **Culture**: Celebrated for fearless student union activism, open-air political debates, ideological congresses, and theatrical street comedy.
* **Key Facility**: **Awo Cafe**—operating late into the night, famous campus-wide for generous portions of jollof rice, beans, dodo, and meat.
* **Curfew**: Open 24/7 for residents.

#### 4. Fajuyi Hall ("Faj" - Male Returning Undergraduates)
* **Designation**: Male Returning Undergraduates.
* **Features**: Houses the **Fajuyi Bus Stop** (a major transit terminal) and a bustling commercial buttery strip offering photocopying, clothing laundry, fried eggs/noodles, and shawarma.
* **Curfew**: Open 24/7.

#### 5. Moremi Hall (Female Returning Undergraduates)
* **Designation**: Female Returning Undergraduates.
* **Location**: Central Academic Border (adjacent to Motion Ground and Hezekiah Library).
* **Advantage**: The most sought-after female hostel due to its walking distance to the library (under 3 minutes), eliminating transit costs for night reading.
* **Curfew**: Main gates locked at 10:00 PM.

#### 6. ETF Hall (Education Trust Fund Hall)
* **Designation**: Male Undergraduates.
* **Structure**: Multi-story modern residential block near Fajuyi Hall with structured room layouts.

#### 7. Alumni Hall
* **Designation**: Female Undergraduates.
* **Location**: Northern residential axis near Road 1 sports corridors. Modern blocks with paved courtyards.

#### 8. Ladoke Akintola Hall
* **Designation**: Female Undergraduates.
* **Location**: Northern residential quadrant near Alumni Hall.

#### 9. Murtala Muhammed Hall
* **Designation**: Mixed-Gender Postgraduate Scholars.
* **Atmosphere**: Quiet, studious environment with dedicated study carrels.

## 2. Bed Space Balloting: The Algorithmic Mechanics

1. **Eligibility Filter**:
   - The balloting engine on `eportal.oauife.edu.ng` remains **locked** until a student's full school charges are verified and stamped by Remita.
2. **Quota Depletion**:
   - Over **8,000 freshmen** compete for fewer than **3,000 available beds**.
   - Available bed spaces are typically claimed within **3 to 5 minutes** of the portal opening.
3. **The 72-Hour Forfeiture Rule**:
   - Once a bed space is locked, a countdown timer starts. The student must pay the designated hostel maintenance fee and upload payment proof within **72 hours**.
   - Failure to complete payment within 72 hours triggers an automatic database release, returning the bed space to the general public pool.
4. **Zero Tolerance for Bed Space Racketeering**:
   - Bed spaces are non-transferable. Buying or selling bed spaces outside the portal violates university regulations and leads to immediate expulsion by the Student Disciplinary Committee.

## 4. Landlord Literacy & Anti-Dupe Protection Rules

1. **The Physical Walkthrough Rule**: Never transfer funds or payment deposits to any individual on WhatsApp, Telegram, or Facebook without an in-person physical inspection accompanied by a senior student or friend.
2. **Water Sourcing Audit**: Test whether water is supplied by an electric borehole connected to an alternative generator or if it depends on an open hand-drawn well that dries up between December and March.
3. **Electricity Billing Transparency**: Confirm whether the house has an individual prepaid meter (sub-metered per flat) or is billed on an estimated general monthly tariff.
4. **Hidden Levies Clause**: Explicitly ask for security association dues, sanitation/waste levies, and caution fee refund policies before signing tenancy documents.
5. **Agent Commission Cap**: Standard real estate agent inspection fees in Ile-Ife should not exceed ₦2,000–₦3,000, and agent commission is standardly capped at **10% to 15%** of the annual rent.
