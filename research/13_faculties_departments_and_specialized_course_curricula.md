# 🏛️ Faculties, Departments & Specialized Course Curricula Matrix

> **"At Obafemi Awolowo University, academic identity is fiercely tribal and meticulously structured. Understanding how 13 faculties, over 60 departments, and specialized sub-disciplines (mini-departments) interconnect is the ultimate key to academic navigation, course registration, and career trajectory."**  
> *Living Research Dossier 13 — OAUMods Information HUB Architecture*

---

## 🧭 1. Executive Landscape: The Academic Architecture

Obafemi Awolowo University's academic structure comprises:
*   **One Collegiate System**: The **College of Health Sciences (CHS)**, housing three professional healthcare faculties.
*   **Ten Autonomous Academic & Professional Faculties**.
*   **The Faculty of Computing Science & Engineering**: The newly unbundled computing ecosystem transitioning from the Faculty of Technology.
*   **The Postgraduate College**: Administering higher degrees across all disciplines.
*   **Over 60 Academic Departments**: Offering more than **100 distinct undergraduate degree pathways, professional options, and sub-specialization tracks**.

### 5. Faculty of Clinical Sciences (College of Health Sciences)
*   **Location**: Pre-clinical training on Campus; Clinical training at **OAUTHC (Teaching Hospitals Complex)**.
*   **Degree Awarded**: **MBBS (Bachelor of Medicine, Bachelor of Surgery)** — 6-Year Professional Program.
*   **Clinical Departments**:
    1.  **Medicine**: Sub-specialties in Cardiology, Nephrology, Gastroenterology, Neurology, Pulmonology, Endocrinology, Infectious Diseases.
    2.  **Surgery**: General Surgery, Orthopaedics & Traumatology, Neurosurgery, Urology, Cardiothoracic Surgery, Paediatric Surgery, Plastic & Reconstructive Surgery.
    3.  **Paediatrics & Child Health**: Neonatology, Paediatric Cardiology, Emergency Paediatrics, Nutritional Paediatrics.
    4.  **Obstetrics, Gynaecology & Perinatology**: Maternal-Fetal Medicine, Reproductive Endocrinology, Gynaecologic Oncology.
    5.  **Community Health & Epidemiology**: Biostatistics, Occupational Health, Environmental Health, Primary Healthcare Administration.
    6.  **Mental Health (Psychiatry)**: General Adult Psychiatry, Child & Adolescent Psychiatry, Substance Abuse Rehabilitation.
    7.  **Anaesthesia & Intensive Care**: Critical Care Medicine, Perioperative Anaesthesiology, Pain Management.
    8.  **Radiology**: Diagnostic Ultrasonography, Computed Tomography (CT), Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI), Interventional Radiology.
    9.  **Dermatology & Venereology**: Clinical Dermatology, Leprosy & Mycobacterial Diseases, Sexually Transmitted Infections.
    10. **Ophthalmology**: Cataract & Refractive Surgery, Glaucoma, Paediatric Ophthalmology.
    11. **Otorhinolaryngology (ENT)**: Otology, Rhinology, Laryngology, Head & Neck Surgery.

### Spotlight A: Department of Sociology & Anthropology (Faculty of Social Sciences)
The Department of Sociology & Anthropology at OAU is celebrated as a unique multidisciplinary powerhouse because it incubated and domiciled **Communication and Media Studies** within the Faculty of Social Sciences.

1.  **B.Sc. Sociology & Anthropology (SOC Major)**:
    *   *Course Prefix*: `SOC`
    *   *Core Disciplines*: Social Stratification, Classical & Modern Sociological Theory, Ethnography, Criminology & Penology, Sociology of Health & Illness, Political Sociology.
2.  **B.Sc. Mass Communication (MCM)**:
    *   *Course Prefix*: `MCM`
    *   *Core Disciplines*: Theories of Mass Communication, Print Journalism, Media Management, Public Relations Strategies, Corporate Advertising, Media Law & Defamation.
3.  **B.Sc. Broadcast Journalism (BCJ)**:
    *   *Course Prefix*: `BCJ`
    *   *Signature Course*: `OAU-BCJ 105` (Introduction to Broadcast Production).
    *   *Core Disciplines*: Radio Scripting & Audio Mixing, Television News Production, On-Air Presentation, Electronic Field Production (EFP), Broadcast Station Administration.
4.  **B.Sc. Information Science & Media Studies (ISMS)**:
    *   *Course Prefix*: `ISMS`
    *   *Core Disciplines*: Digital Media Publishing, Web Informatics, Data-Driven Journalism, Human-Computer Interaction, Search Engine Strategies, Media Analytics.
5.  **B.Sc. Film Production & Multimedia Studies (FMM)**:
    *   *Course Prefix*: `FMM`
    *   *Core Disciplines*: Screenwriting & Storyboarding, Cinematography, Digital Non-Linear Editing (NLE), Film Directing, Sound Design for Motion Pictures, Documentary Production.

> [!NOTE]
> **The Social Sciences Foundation Requirement**: Because MCM, BCJ, ISMS, and FMM are housed inside the **Faculty of Social Sciences** (rather than Arts), all communication students at OAU are required to pass **foundational mathematical and quantitative courses**—specifically **SSC 105 (Mathematics for Social Scientists)** or **MTH 105**—alongside introductory Economics (`ECN 101`), Political Science (`POL 101`), Psychology (`PSY 101`), and Demography (`DSS 101`).

### Spotlight B: Department of Dramatic Arts (Faculty of Arts)
Operating out of the historic **Pit Theatre** (designed by Sharon with amphitheatre-style sunken staging), the Department of Dramatic Arts provides five specialized terminal practical options in Part 3 and Part 4:
1.  **Playwriting & Dramatic Literature**: Script creation, dramaturgy, post-colonial African theatrical texts.
2.  **Directing & Stage Management**: Theatrical interpretation, blocking, actor coaching, technical rehearsal coordination.
3.  **Technical Theatre**: Lighting plots, acoustic amplification, costume design, stage makeup, set construction.
4.  **Dance & Choreography**: Traditional Yoruba festive dance, African kinetic aesthetics, contemporary dance composition.
5.  **Media & Motion Picture Production**: Radio/TV drama, screen acting, video film editing.

### Spotlight C: Faculty of Agriculture Integrated Model
The Faculty of Agriculture operates an integrated pyramid where students do not isolate into departmental silos until their final year:
*   **Part 1 (Freshmen)**: Pure foundation sciences in the White House (`CHM 101/107`, `PHY 101/107`, `BIO 101/107`, `MTH 101`).
*   **Part 2 & Part 3**: Core agricultural fundamentals (`AGR 201`, `AGR 301` series spanning animal husbandry, crop husbandry, soil physics, and agricultural economics).
*   **Part 4 (Farm Practical Year - FPY)**: Zero classroom examinations. Students spend an entire academic session on the **OAU Teaching & Research Farm** managing crop plots, broilers, layers, swine, and dairy cattle, earning hands-on agricultural credits.
*   **Part 5 (Terminal Departmental Specialization)**: Students branch into their final departmental concentrations (Agric Economics, Animal Science, Crop Protection, Soil Science, or Agric Extension) to execute their final degree dissertations.

## 💡 5. Freshman Action Guide for Faculty Navigation

1.  **Locate Your Faculty Office on Day 1**: Do not wait for clearance week. Visit your Faculty Officer's desk to verify the approved course list for your specific department.
2.  **Confirm Your Exact Course Code Prefixes**: Note whether your courses carry general faculty codes (e.g. `SSC 105`, `SER 001`, `LIB 001`) or departmental codes (`SOC`, `MCM`, `BCJ`, `CHM`, `MTH`, `EEE`).
3.  **Buy the Authentic Departmental Handbook**: Every department publishes an official handbook detailing credit unit limits, prerequisite trees (e.g., you cannot take `MTH 201` without passing `MTH 101`), and graduation requirements.
4.  **Know Your Faculty File Jacket Color**: Submitting documents in the wrong color folder causes immediate administrative rejection during physical screening.
