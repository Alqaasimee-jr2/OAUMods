# 🚌 Campus Transit Mobility, Green Fleet & Fare Tariff Engine

> **"At Obafemi Awolowo University, campus transit is a regulated municipal ecosystem. Operating across a vast 13,000-acre estate with distinct zoning, daily student mobility relies on a structured fare system, electric tricycles, CNG vehicles, and dedicated transit corridors."**

## 🧭 1. Executive Landscape: The Great Ife Transit System

Campus transportation at Obafemi Awolowo University is managed under the oversight of the **University Transportation Committee**, the **Division of Student Affairs (DSA)**, and student union transport subcommittees. 

### Key Contemporary Paradigm Shifts:
1. **The Green Mobility Transition**:
   * OAU is progressively phasing in **Electric Tricycles (E-Trikes)** and **Compressed Natural Gas (CNG) buses/shuttles** to combat fuel price volatility, cut emissions, and provide cleaner campus commuting.
2. **Upcoming Tariff Review (October 1st Milestone)**:
   * A revised tariff structure is scheduled to take effect from **October 1st**. The figures documented in this dossier represent the active, verified baseline rates preceding the October 1 adjustment.
3. **The Universal "Ticket" Currency**:
   * Physical cash is **not** handed directly to drivers for internal bus and tricycle transit. Instead, campus mobility operates on a **custom-made, laminated ticket voucher system**.

## 🎟️ 2. The Custom Laminated Ticket System

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ THE GOLDEN RULE OF OAU TRANSIT TICKETS:                                          │
│                                                                                  │
│                     1 TICKET = ₦100 (ONE HUNDRED NAIRA)                          │
│                                                                                  │
│ ⚠️ NEVER LET ANYONE SCAM YOU INTO PAYING ₦200 FOR A SINGLE TICKET.               │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 1. Physical Specifications & Anti-Counterfeiting:
* **Format**: Heavyweight, laminated custom-printed cardstock.
* **Authentication Feature**: Every authentic university transit ticket carries a **special affixed paper sticker / validation stamp** applied to the laminated body. Always check for this seal to ensure you have not been handed invalid or cloned counterfeit tickets.

### 2. Purchasing Points & Payment Methods:
* **Accepted Payment Modes**: **Cash** or **Instant Bank Transfer** at the ticket booths.
* **Official Ticket Booth Locations**:
  1. **Campus Main Gate Terminal**: The primary ticket depot where commuters buy tickets immediately upon entering through the Main Gate.
  2. **Halls of Residence Terminals**: Dedicated ticket points located at each major hall (Angola, Mozambique, Fajuyi, Awolowo, Moremi, ETF).
  3. **New Market Terminal**: Ticket point situated within the OAU Central Market.
* **Operational Caution**:
  * Ticket points are **rarely available at lecture halls or faculty buildings**. Freshmen must make it a habit to purchase multiple tickets (e.g. ₦1,000–₦2,000 worth) at the Gate or Halls in advance to avoid being stranded when heading back from lectures.

Tricycles are the primary transit mode for reaching **faculties, lecture theatres, and deeper academic zones** where large buses cannot navigate.

```
┌───────────────────────────────────────┬────────────┬─────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Route (Tricycle / Keke / E-Trike)     │ Tickets    │ Naira Value │ Key Practical Advice                     │
├───────────────────────────────────────┼────────────┼─────────────┼──────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ **Campus Gate $\rightarrow$ SUB / Bus Stop**  │ 2 Tickets  │ **₦200**    │ Direct drop-off at SUB central terminal  │
│ **Campus Gate $\rightarrow$ Halls of Residence**│ 3 Tickets│ **₦300**    │ Goes directly to Angola, Moz, Faj, Awo   │
│ **Campus Gate $\rightarrow$ New Market**      │ 3 Tickets  │ **₦300**    │ Direct drop-off at Central Market gate   │
│ **Campus Gate $\rightarrow$ Lecture Theatres**│ 3 Tickets  │ **₦300**    │ Covers BOOC, White House, Yellow House   │
│ **Campus Gate $\rightarrow$ Faculties**       │ 3 Tickets  │ **₦300**    │ Direct to Tech, EDM, Law, Admin, Arts    │
│ **Campus Gate $\rightarrow$ ICT (INTECU)**    │ 3 Tickets  │ **₦300**    │ Direct drop-off at INTECU corridor       │
└───────────────────────────────────────┴────────────┴─────────────┴──────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

> [!TIP]
> **Why Tricycles Are Indispensable for Academics**: Campus shuttle buses almost **never** enter the inner academic avenues leading to individual faculty quads and lecture theatres. If your lecture is at BOOC, White House, or Spider House and you have luggage or limited time, take a **tricycle (3 tickets = ₦300)**.

### Special Religious Transit Operations:
* On standard weekdays, buses running between Halls and Religious Ground are rare.
* **Friday Juma'at**: Dedicated buses run between Halls of Residence and the **Central Mosque / Religious Ground** for 2 tickets (₦200).
* **Sunday Christian Services**: High-volume bus fleets run from Halls to the **Religious Ground auditoriums** (ECU, RCF, DLCF, All Souls, etc.) and back for 2 tickets (₦200).

## 🏥 6. OAUTHC (Teaching Hospital) Transit Corridor

Transit to the **Obafemi Awolowo University Teaching Hospitals Complex (OAUTHC)** on Ilesa Road operates under a distinct protocol separate from the internal ticket ecosystem.

```
                      [ ON-CAMPUS COMMUTER ]
                                 │
                                 ▼
           [ PROCEED TO COLLEGE OF HEALTH SCIENCES (CHS) ]
                                 │
                                 ▼
           [ BOARD DESIGNATED "MICRA" TOWN SHUTTLES ]
                                 │
      ┌──────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────┐
      ▼                                                     ▼
[ PAYMENT: ₦300 CASH ONLY ]                  [ DESTINATION: OAUTHC & GATE ]
(Tickets NOT accepted on Micras)             (Direct drop-off at Hospital)
```

### The OAUTHC Transit Rules:
1. **Departure Point on Campus**: Commuters proceed to the **College of Health Sciences (CHS)** complex car park along **Road 2** (Academic Core).
   > [!IMPORTANT]
   > **Crucial Geographic Distinction**: The **College of Health Sciences (CHS)** is **NOT** close to or at the **University Health Centre ("JAC")**. They are located in completely different parts of the campus. The Health Centre is located in the student residential hostel sector (opposite Alumni Hall, beside Awolowo Hall), whereas the College of Health Sciences is an academic collegiate complex located along Road 2 (adjacent to Pharmacy and EDM). Commuters heading to OAUTHC must board the Nissan Micra cabs at the **College of Health Sciences (CHS) park along Road 2**, NOT at the Health Centre!
2. **Vehicle Fleet**: Operated by **Nissan Micra** vehicles.
3. **Fare**: **₦300 Flat Rate** per passenger.
4. **Payment Mode**: **CASH ONLY**. *Tickets are strictly NOT accepted on the Micra shuttles*.
5. **Return Trip**: Boarding from the OAUTHC complex / THC Gate back to campus is also **₦300 cash**.

## 💡 7. Freshman Transit Survival Rules

1. **Keep a Reserve of Tickets in Your Wallet**: Always purchase 10–20 tickets (₦1,000–₦2,000) at the Campus Gate or Hall buttery ticket booths. Being stranded in an academic area without tickets forces long treks.
2. **Inspect the Ticket Seal**: Verify the small paper sticker / authenticity stamp affixed to each laminated ticket.
3. **Know Vehicle Limitations**:
   * Taking a bus to a lecture hall will likely drop you at the main road, requiring a 10-minute walk.
   * If you need direct delivery to BOOC, Spider House, or Yellow House, take a **tricycle (3 tickets = ₦300)**.
4. **Prepare for October 1st Changes**: Keep abreast of university circulars regarding updated tariff schedules starting from next month.
