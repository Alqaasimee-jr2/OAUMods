## 🧭 1. Executive Summary & Verification Rules

At Great Ife, campus geography is celebrated for its **Bauhaus-inspired tropical modernist architecture** designed by Israeli architect **Arieh Sharon**, his son **Eldar Sharon**, and Nigerian associate **A. A. Egbor**. Over sixty years of vibrant student life, distinctive architectural forms have generated iconic nicknames that serve as primary spatial navigation coordinates.

### The Forensic Accuracy Protocol
To ensure zero misinformation reaches incoming freshers, every landmark, building nickname, and spatial moniker in this dossier has been verified against:
1. **Architectural Blueprints & Monographs** (*Arieh Sharon: Planning Jerusalem and the University of Ife*).
2. **University Departmental Geographies & Faculty Handbooks**.
3. **Verified Campus Journalism Archives** (*Association of Campus Journalists - ACJ OAU*, *OAU Peeps*, *An-Nuur Press*).
4. **Myth-Busting Verification**: Specifically clarifying widespread rumors and misattributions (such as confusing the Mathematics building with Social Sciences, or placing town monuments on campus).

## 🏛️ 2. The Big Three: Iconic Building Nicknames

---

### 1. ⚪ "White House" — The Faculty of Science Central Complex

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ OFFICIAL NAME      : Faculty of Science Complex / Central Science Laboratories    │
│ NICKNAME           : "White House"                                               │
│ PRIMARY OCCUPANTS  : Faculty of Science Deanship, Chemistry & Physics Departments│
│ LOCATION           : Central Academic Zone (Directly adjacent to Yellow House)   │
│ ARCHITECTURAL MOTIF: Bauhaus Tropical Modernist white board-marked concrete      │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

#### Why It Is Called "White House":
* **The Visual Origin**: The building features a massive, sprawling white-painted concrete facade with sweeping linear horizontal sunshades (brise-soleil) and stark white walls contrasting with dark slate accents. 
* **The Academic Significance**: For Great Ife science, engineering, and healthcare students, the White House is the epicentre of academic toil ("academic hustle"). It houses the Faculty Dean's Office, the Dean's Board Room, massive undergraduate Chemistry and Physics laboratories, and core lecture amphitheatres including AUD II.
* **Corridor Culture**: Its long, breezy open-air corridors are perpetually packed with students waiting for laboratory practicals, engaging in heated tutorial arguments, or reading on stone benches during exam revision weeks.

---

### 2. 🟡 "Yellow House" — The Department of Mathematics Building

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ OFFICIAL NAME      : Department of Mathematics Building                          │
│ NICKNAME           : "Yellow House"                                              │
│ PRIMARY OCCUPANTS  : Department of Mathematics (Faculty of Science)              │
│ LOCATION           : Science Quadrangle (Adjacent to White House & Moremi Hall)  │
│ ARCHITECTURAL MOTIF: Deep Ochre / Sunshine Yellow Exterior Masonry               │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

#### Why It Is Called "Yellow House":
* **The Visual Origin**: The building is painted in an unmistakable, vibrant sunny yellow / ochre hue, standing out in sharp contrast against the pristine white of the neighboring White House and the green foliage of the campus central gardens.
* **The Academic Significance**: It serves as the administrative and lecture base for the Department of Mathematics. Almost every freshman in Science, Technology, Education, and Social Sciences treks to the Yellow House for mathematics tutorials, departmental quiz competitions, calculus problem-solving sessions, and notice board results.

> [!CAUTION]
> **Myth-Buster #1 — Yellow House vs. Social Sciences**:
> Freshers frequently mistake the **Faculty of Social Sciences Building** (which has yellow/ochre accent panels facing the Senate Building) for "Yellow House." On campus, **"Yellow House" specifically and exclusively designates the Mathematics Department building**. Social Sciences is simply referred to as *"Faculty of Social Sciences"* or *"Social Sciences Complex."*

### 3. 🕷️ "Spider House" — The Civil Engineering & Technology Complex

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ OFFICIAL NAME      : Civil Engineering Building / Faculty of Technology Foyer    │
│ NICKNAME           : "Spider House"                                              │
│ PRIMARY OCCUPANTS  : Civil, Mechanical & Materials Science Engineering Depts     │
│ LOCATION           : Technology Belt (Road 1, directly east of White House)      │
│ ARCHITECTURAL MOTIF: Cantilevered diagonal steel trusses & external stilt legs   │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

#### Why It Is Called "Spider House":
* **The Visual Origin**: Designed in the 1970s by Arieh and Eldar Sharon in partnership with A. A. Egbor, the building features an extraordinary structural engineering feat: its primary structural steel trusses and diagonal support beams project outward from the roof and sides down to the foundation, visually mimicking the angled legs of a giant mechanical arachnid crouching over the complex.
* **The Academic Significance**: It houses the heavy structural and hydraulic laboratories of the Department of Civil Engineering, alongside mechanical workshops and materials testing suites.
* **Transit Landmark**: In campus transportation, "Spider House" is a universally recognized shuttle drop-off and pickup point along the academic shuttle circuit.

## 🎭 3. Legendary Halls, Auditoriums & Lecture Theatres

---

### 4. 🧬 "BOOC" — Biological Sciences Lecture Theatre C

* **Pronunciation**: *"Bee-Oh-Oh-See"* (never pronounced as "Book").
* **Official Name**: **Biological Sciences Lecture Theatre C** (part of the BOOA, BOOB, and BOOC cluster).
* **Location**: Biological Sciences Complex (Ground Floor, adjacent to the Zoology & Botany corridors).
* **Significance**: 
  * The largest single lecture theatre in the Biological Sciences cluster.
  * It is ground zero for incoming STEM freshers, where foundation courses such as `BIO 101`, `BIO 102`, `CHM 101`, and `GST 101` overflow into the aisles.
  * In January 2022, BOOC, BOOA, BOOB, and Ajose were commissioned after comprehensive modernization and air-conditioning refurbishment donated by Platform Capital.

---

### 5. 🏛️ "ODLT" (ODLT 1 & 2) — Oduduwa Lecture Theatres 1 & 2

* **Official Name**: **Oduduwa Lecture Theatres 1 and 2**.
* **Location**: Directly behind the grand Oduduwa Hall complex, overlooking Motion Ground.
* **Significance**:
  * Massive, raked amphitheatrical lecture halls featuring steeply stepped seating tiers designed to give hundreds of students an unobstructed view of the lecturer's dais.
  * Used for university-wide General Studies (GST), foundational law lectures, and joint faculty examinations.
  * Due to their size, ODLT 1 & 2 are prime landmarks during student election manifesto nights and faculty congresses.

---

### 6. 🌍 "Afrika Amphitheatre" ("Amphi") — The Open-Air Bowl of Freedom

* **Official Name**: **Afrika Amphitheatre** (formerly Central Amphitheatre).
* **Capacity**: 5,000+ open-air stepped amphitheatre bowl.
* **Location**: Built into the sunken natural slope beneath Oduduwa Hall and the Humanities terrace.
* **Why It Was Renamed**:
  * Renamed in eternal memory of **George Akinyemi Iwilade**, the charismatic 21-year-old 400-Level Law student and Secretary-General of the Great Ife Students' Union, universally known by his aluta moniker **"Afrika."**
  * Afrika was assassinated in the tragic, notorious pre-dawn cultist attack of **July 10, 1999**, alongside four other student martyrs (the "OAU Five").
* **Role in Campus Life**:
  * The symbolic and spiritual sanctuary of Great Ife student resistance and intellectual democracy.
  * Host to the historic matriculation addresses, SUG presidential inauguration ceremonies, university convocation overflow, annual July 10 commemorative symposiums, and major gospel/musical concerts.

---

### 7. 🎭 "Pit Theatre" — The Sunken Dramaturgical Laboratory

* **Official Name**: **The Pit Theatre** (Department of Dramatic Arts Performance Theatre).
* **Location**: Faculty of Arts Complex (Humanities Block A courtyard).
* **Architectural Origin**:
  * Designed by Arieh Sharon as a revolutionary sunken arena / theatre-in-the-round. The performance stage sits at the lowest floor level in a literal "pit," surrounded by concentric stepped concrete tiers.
  * This sunken configuration creates raw, unbroken physical intimacy between actors and the audience, eliminating the elitist distance of traditional Western proscenium stages.
* **Cultural Pedigree**:
  * The stage where literary and theatrical giants—including **Prof. Wole Soyinka** (Nobel Laureate) and **Prof. Ola Rotimi**—directed groundbreaking indigenous plays and established the legendary "Ife Film & Theatre Tradition."
  * Regularly stages departmental productions, convocation drama festivals, student variety shows, and artistic symposiums.

---

### 8. 📜 "Motion Ground" — The Concourse of Aluta

* **Official Name**: Central Campus Concourse / Library Esplanade.
* **Location**: The expansive paved pedestrian quadrangle framed between the **Hezekiah Oluwasanmi Library**, **Oduduwa Hall**, the **Ken Saro-Wiwa Students' Union Building (SUB)**, and the Senate Building.
* **Why It Is Called "Motion Ground"**:
  * The name originates from university unionist history: it is the open-air floor where student motions, resolutions, and political actions are initiated, debated, and set into physical motion before the student body.
  * It represents the central pedestrian and philosophical crossroads of the campus. Every mass peaceful march, victory parade, political rally, and post-congress demonstration converges at Motion Ground.
  * In campus parlance, a student who spends all day socializing or wandering without attending lectures is humorously chided as *"loitering on Motion Ground."*

---

### 9. ✊ "SUB" — The Ken Saro-Wiwa Students' Union Building

* **Official Name**: **Ken Saro-Wiwa Building** (Students' Union Building).
* **Location**: Central Campus, directly framing the southern perimeter of Motion Ground.
* **Historical Dedication**:
  * Named in honor of Nigerian environmental activist, writer, and Ogoni leader **Ken Saro-Wiwa** (1941–1995).
  * The remodeled modern edifice was officially commissioned on **November 12, 2022**, funded through a landmark intervention by former Chief of Defence Staff and Great Ife alumnus, **General Lucky Irabor**.
* **Role in Campus Life**:
  * Regarded by the student body as the **"Seat of Aluta."**
  * Houses the Central Executive Council (CEC) secretariat, the Students' Representative Council (SRC) parliamentary chambers, the Students' Judicial Council (SJC), campus buttery kiosks, Union stationery stores, and the SUB television lounge.

## 🏘️ 4. Residential, Social & Commercial Nicknames

---

### 10. 💘 "Anglomoz" & "Moz 101" — The Freshmen Courting Corridor

* **Official Location**: The open paved concourse, car park, and pedestrian lane situated directly between **Angola Hall** (all-male freshman hall) and **Mozambique Hall** (all-female freshman hall).
* **The Culture of Anglomoz**:
  * Because Great Ife strictly segregates 100-level accommodation into Angola and Moz, the space connecting both halls naturally evolved into the bustling social epicenter for freshmen interactions, evening strolls, and camaraderie.
  * Famous for late-night fast food stalls (indomie, roasted plantain, toasted bread, and suya).
* **"Moz 101"**:
  * The legendary campus slang referring to the comedic "course" of a male freshman learning the art of wooing, courting, and waiting for female students outside the gates of Mozambique Hall.
  * Upperclassmen often tease timid freshers with: *"Have you registered and passed Moz 101 yet?"*

---

### 11. 🛒 "New Market" (OAU Central Market)

* **Official Name**: **OAU Central Market**.
* **Location**: Residential Zone (Situated directly behind ETF Hall and accessible via the road between Adekunle Fajuyi Hall and ETF).
* **Significance**:
  * The university's bustling one-stop commercial hub.
  * Houses hundreds of independent student-friendly vendors: raw foodstuffs, groceries, fresh fruits, clothing boutiques, phone/gadget repairs, stationery and printing shops, unisex hair salons, laundries, and tailoring.
  * Houses the famous traditional campus "Bukas" where students go for authentic local meals.

---

### 12. 🍲 "New Buka" (New Bukateria Complex)

* **Official Name**: **New Bukateria Complex**.
* **Location**: Along the residential perimeter road connecting Fajuyi Hall and the Sports Complex.
* **The Buka Culture**:
  * Unlike New Market which sells provisions and goods, New Buka is dedicated exclusively to cooked meals and restaurant dining.
  * Houses beloved campus eateries such as *Mr. Banwill*, *Jo's Indulge*, and *A1 Food Court*.
  * Serves as an institutional equalizer where first-class professors, freshmen, and administrative staff queue side-by-side for hot meals.

---

### 13. 🥪 "Risky" (or "Risky Burger") — The Great Ife Midnight Fuel

* **Category**: Iconic Campus Culinary Slang.
* **What It Is**: A hot, custom street-food snack comprising a spicy fried egg (whipped with onions, scotch bonnet peppers, and seasoning cubes) pressed inside a freshly baked, warm miniature loaf of soft Agege bread.
* **Cultural Mythos**:
  * Revered as the ultimate budget-friendly "life-saver" during all-night examination reading marathons ("TDB" — Till Day Break).
  * Freshmen are warned that eating a roadside "Risky" from a night vendor is an unofficial campus rite of passage.

---

### 14. 🚰 "Subair" — The Campus Water Sanctuary

* **Official Name**: Central Campus Borehole & Water Treatment Annex.
* **Location**: Proximity to Adekunle Fajuyi Hall and Moremi Hall residential fringe.
* **Significance**:
  * When municipal or campus-wide pipe water distribution faces electrical interruptions, the Subair borehole and storage tanks remain the most reliable gravity-fed spring on campus.
  * Etched into Great Ife history during the famous **2023 "Subair Wash-a-thon,"** where students gathered en masse with buckets, turning laundry day during a water drought into an unforgettable display of collective solidarity and Aroism humor.

---

### 15. 🐚 "The Leventis Edifice" — Natural History Museum

* **Official Name**: **The Natural History Museum of Obafemi Awolowo University**.
* **Nicknames**: *"The Eighth Wonder"*, *"The Snail Shell"*, *"The Leventis Building"*.
* **Location**: Adjacent to the Department of Geology and the Sports Complex.
* **Architectural Marvel**:
  * Designed by James Cubitt Architects with sweeping, low-hanging triangular rooflines and dramatic high-relief sculptural concrete mimicking an organic geological fold or snail shell.
  * Construction began in the 1980s and was famously stalled for 34 years due to federal funding shortfalls, until the **A. G. Leventis Foundation** provided an extraordinary multimillion-naira grant to complete and inaugurate the world-class museum in **2011**.
  * It remains the only dedicated university-based natural history museum in Nigeria.

## 🚫 5. Myth-Busters: Widespread Nickname Misconceptions

| Rumor / Misconception | Forensic Reality Check & Truth |
| :--- | :--- |
| **"The Senate Building is called the Glass House."** | ❌ **FALSE.** OAU has no building named "Glass House." That nickname belongs to a lecture hall at *Olabisi Onabanjo University (OOU)* or the *Femi Johnson Broking House* in Ibadan. The administrative tower at OAU is strictly called the **Senate Building**. |
| **"Yellow House is the Faculty of Social Sciences."** | ❌ **FALSE.** On Great Ife campus, **"Yellow House" specifically designates the Department of Mathematics building** (within the Faculty of Science). Social Sciences is simply called the *Social Sciences Building* or *Yellow-Panel Building*. |
| **"The Health Centre is named JAC."** | ❌ **FALSE.** The campus primary clinic is the **University Health Centre**. **"JAC"** stands for the **Joint Action Committee**, the coalition of university non-teaching unions (NASU, SSANU, NAAT) that leads campus strikes and industrial actions. |
| **"The College of Health Sciences (CHS) is at or near the Health Centre."** | ❌ **FALSE.** The **College of Health Sciences (CHS)** is an academic collegiate complex located along **Road 2** in the academic sciences quadrangle (housing Anatomy, Physiology, Medical Biochemistry, Dentistry, and clinical offices, adjacent to Pharmacy and EDM). The **University Health Centre** is a primary clinical facility located in the **residential hall sector** (opposite Alumni Hall, beside Awolowo Hall). They are located in completely different parts of campus and serve entirely different functions. Shuttles to OAUTHC load at CHS along Road 2, not at the Health Centre. |
| **"The ancient Staff of Oranmiyan (Opa Oranmiyan) is on campus."** | ❌ **FALSE.** The ancient 5.5-meter granite monolith of *Opa Oranmiyan* is located in the **ancient town of Ile-Ife** (in the Mopa/Arubidi quarter). The 1960s proposal to build a campus replica was officially rejected. |
| **"BOOC is a library reading room."** | ❌ **FALSE.** **BOOC** stands for **Biological Sciences Lecture Theatre C**, a massive lecture theatre in the Faculty of Science used primarily for large 100-level STEM foundation classes. |
