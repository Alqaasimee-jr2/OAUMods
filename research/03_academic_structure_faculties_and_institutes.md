## 1. The Collegiate & Faculty Governance Model

Obafemi Awolowo University operates an expansive academic model structured into:
* **One Collegiate System**: The **College of Health Sciences** (coordinating three distinct medical faculties).
* **Ten Autonomous Professional and Academic Faculties**.
* **The Postgraduate College**: Administering advanced research degrees across all academic disciplines.
* **Over 60 Academic Departments**.
* **Seven Specialized National and Regional Institutes of Excellence**.

## 3. Specialized Autonomous Institutes & Centers of Excellence

1. **Centre for Energy Research and Development (CERD)**:
   - A federal institute of nuclear science and technology located on campus.
   - Operates a tandem accelerator, neutron generators, and advanced laboratories for nuclear physics, radiation biophysics, and environmental monitoring.
2. **National Centre for Technology Management (NACETEM)**:
   - Federal agency providing policy research and capacity building in Science, Technology, and Innovation (STI) management.
3. **Regional Centre for Training in Aerospace Surveys (RECTAS)**:
   - Established in 1972 under the auspices of the United Nations Economic Commission for Africa (UNECA).
   - Serves African nations in geoinformatics, satellite remote sensing, photogrammetry, and cartography.
4. **African Regional Centre for Space Science and Technology Education in English (ARCSSTE-E)**:
   - Affiliated with the United Nations Office for Outer Space Affairs (UNOOSA), training engineers across Africa in satellite meteorology, space communications, and remote sensing.
5. **Information Technology and Communications Unit (INTECU)**:
   - Pioneers of the campus-wide fiber-optic broadband backbone and the Great Ife ePortal software engine.
6. **Natural History Museum of Nigeria (A.G. Leventis Complex)**:
   - Autonomous national scientific repository housing over 100,000 botanical, geological, archaeological, and zoological holotypes.
7. **Obafemi Awolowo University Teaching Hospitals Complex (OAUTHC)**:
   - The clinical teaching hospital arm of the university.
   - Operating units:
     * *Ifẹ Hospital Unit (IHUC)* along Ilesa Road, Ile-Ife.
     * *Wesley Guild Hospital* in Ilesa.
     * *Comprehensive Health Centres* in Imesi-Ile and Enuwa.
     * Renowned globally for pioneering Nigeria’s first successful separation of Siamese conjoined twins and renal transplant surgery.
