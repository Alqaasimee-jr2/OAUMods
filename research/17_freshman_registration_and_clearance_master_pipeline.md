## 🏥 2. Health Registration: University Health Centre ("JAC")

### Spatial Orientation & Triage Reality
*   **Location**: University Health Centre (Situated in the residential hostel sector, directly **opposite Alumni Hall and beside Awolowo Hall**).
*   **Operating Hours**: Screening unit operates Monday to Friday, **8:00 AM – 3:30 PM**.
*   **Triage Tally Reality**: Morning queues start forming as early as **6:00 AM**. Records staff distribute numbered paper tallies starting at **7:00 AM**. Arriving after 8:30 AM typically means your tally will be numbered in the 200s or rolled over to the following day.

### Prerequisite Document Dossier:
1.  **Student Medical Screening Form**: Downloaded from `eportal.oauife.edu.ng` and printed in duplicate.
2.  **Chest X-Ray Film & Official Radiologist Report**: Obtained from OAUTHC or an accredited Federal/State Teaching Hospital. Must be in an X-ray envelope with the radiologist's official stamp.
3.  **Laboratory Investigation Results**:
    *   Blood Group certification.
    *   Genotype (Hb electrophoresis) certification.
    *   Packed Cell Volume (PCV) / Complete Blood Count.
    *   Urinalysis report.
4.  **Immunization / Vaccination Record**: Showing basic tetanus and routine vaccines.
5.  **Two (2) Recent Passport Photographs**: Strictly with a **Red background**.

### Step-by-Step Screening Pipeline:
```
[ Tally Collection ] ➔ [ Vitals & Nursing Triage ] ➔ [ Lab & X-Ray Audit ] ➔ [ Doctor's Exam ] ➔ [ Green Card Issuance ]
   (6:30–7:30 AM)         (BP, Weight, Vision)           (Report Vetting)       (Consultation)        (Permanent Medical ID)
```

1.  **Step 1: Tally Number Assignment**: Collect your daily paper tally at the Health Centre entrance security desk.
2.  **Step 2: Nursing Vitals Assessment**: Nursing staff measure blood pressure, pulse, body weight, height, and perform a basic Snellen chart visual acuity test.
3.  **Step 3: Diagnostic Laboratory Cross-Check**: Medical laboratory scientists review your lab printouts and verify concordance with clinical records.
4.  **Step 4: Medical Officer Clinical Consultation**: A designated campus physician conducts a physical assessment (chest auscultation, abdominal palpation, medical history review) and signs the physician certification section.
5.  **Step 5: Laminating & Issuing the "Green Card"**: The Medical Records unit registers your bio-data into the university health database and issues your laminated **University Health Centre Patient Card ("Green Card")**.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **The Green Card Lifeline**: Your Green Card guarantees **100% free primary consultations, emergency admissions, routine laboratory investigations, and essential prescription medications** throughout your undergraduate years at OAU. Never leave it in your home state; keep it accessible in your hostel room.

## 🛏️ 3. Hall of Residence Registration & Check-In

### Primary Enclaves for Freshmen:
*   **Male Freshmen (100 Level)**: Exclusively accommodated in **Angola Hall**.
*   **Female Freshmen (100 Level)**: Exclusively accommodated in **Mozambique Hall ("Moz")**.

### Prerequisite Document Dossier:
1.  **Hostel Allocation Slip**: Original printout from the ePortal showing your assigned room number and bunk allocation.
2.  **Remita Accommodation Payment Receipt**: Bank-stamped or online payment confirmation showing payment within the mandatory 72-hour balloting payment window.
3.  **Two (2) Red-Background Passport Photographs**.
4.  **Photocopy of JAMB Admission Letter & OAU Acceptance Receipt**.
5.  **One Flat File Jacket (Standard Pale Yellow)**.

### Step-by-Step Check-In Pipeline:
1.  **Hall Warden Verification Desk**: Present your ePortal bedspace allocation slip. The Hall Warden's administrative staff verify your matric number/JAMB reg against the master hall manifest.
2.  **Payment of Hall Association Dues**: Pay the mandatory Hall Executive dues (₦1,500 – ₦2,500 cash or POS) at the Hall Secretariat. Collect your:
    *   Official Hall Handbook & Freshers' Survival Guide.
    *   Custom Hall T-Shirt or customized Hall Sticker.
    *   Official Hall Dues Cleared Receipt.
3.  **Signing the Hall Tenancy & Fire Safety Undertaking**:
    *   **The Appliance Ban**: Students must sign an explicit undertaking pledging **never to bring or operate electric cooking coils, boiling rings, hotplates, or electric toasters** in the rooms. (Kerosene stoves and approved gas camp cylinders are restricted to designated kitchenettes/balconies). Violators face immediate bedspace forfeiture and disciplinary referral.
    *   **Curfew Compliance**: Agreement to the **10:00 PM hostel gate curfew**.
4.  **Porter's Lodge Key Issuance**: The Chief Porter inspects your clearance docket, assigns your physical bunk (Upper or Lower Bunk), registers your mattress, and hands over your physical room door key.

## 🏛️ 4. Faculty Registration & Credential Verification

### The Golden Rule: The Universal Pale Yellow File Jacket
*   **Campus-Wide Standard**: Across all faculties (Science, Tech, Administration, Arts, Social Sciences, EDM, Pharmacy, Basic Medical Sciences, Law, Education, Agriculture, Computing), document screening strictly requires the **Standard Pale Yellow Flat File Jacket**.
*   **Labeling Requirement**: Neatly write in bold black ink on the cover:
    *   Full Legal Name (Surname first in capital letters).
    *   JAMB Registration Number & OAU Matric Number (if assigned).
    *   Faculty & Department.
    *   Academic Session & Contact Phone Number.

### Prerequisite Document Dossier:
```

│ No │ Document Description                                   │ Required Format     │
├────┼────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┼─────────────────────┤

│ 2  │ OAU Post-UTME Screening Result Slip                    │ 2 Photocopies       │
│ 3  │ O'Level Statement of Results / Certificates (WAEC/NECO)│ Original + 3 Copies │
│ 4  │ Birth Certificate or NPC Declaration of Age            │ Original + 3 Copies │
```

### Step-by-Step Faculty Officer (FO) Pipeline:
1.  **Dossier Assembly**: Arrange your documents inside the pale yellow file jacket in the exact order listed on your faculty's physical notice board.
    > [!CAUTION]
    > **NEVER staple original certificates into your file jacket.** Use steel paperclips or transparent acid-free plastic document sleeves. Piercing original certificates with staples can result in rejection or damage.
2.  **Credential Scrutiny**: The Faculty Officer conducts a line-by-line verification:
    *   Letter-for-letter match of your name across JAMB, O'Level, and Birth Certificate.
    *   Verification of minimum 5 O'Level credits in relevant subjects at not more than two sittings.
    *   Online verification of WAEC/NECO scratch card pins.
3.  **Endorsement & Stamping**: The Faculty Officer stamps your clearance eligibility certificate and signs your **Course Registration Eligibility Docket**.
4.  **Archive Retention**: The Faculty retains the pale yellow file jacket containing the verified photocopies as your permanent institutional undergraduate record.

## 📚 5. Departmental Registration & Course Sign-Off

### Office & Spatial Context:
*   **Location**: Your respective Departmental Secretariat (e.g., Computer Science in Spider House; Chemistry in White House; Sociology in Social Sciences Building).
*   **Officials**: Head of Department (HOD) and the designated **100-Level Course Advisor**.

### The Great Ife Credit Load Regulations:
*   **Minimum Credit Load**: **15 Credit Units** per semester.
*   **Maximum Credit Load**: **24 Credit Units** per semester (special senate approval required to exceed).
*   **Course Classification**:
    *   *Compulsory Courses (C)*: Foundational courses that must be registered, taken, and passed to graduate (e.g., `GST 101`, `GST 102`, core departmental courses).
    *   *Required Courses (R)*: Essential courses that must be registered and taken, but a marginal pass/compensation may apply based on departmental handbook rules.
    *   *Elective Courses (E)*: Optional broadening courses selected outside or within your faculty.

### Step-by-Step Departmental Pipeline:
1.  **Course Advisor Review**:
    *   Sit with your assigned 100-Level Course Advisor.
    *   Review your draft course selection from the ePortal to ensure there are no timetable clashes (e.g., two lectures scheduled for the same hour in BOOC and White House).
    *   Confirm that all prerequisites for 200-level courses are captured.
2.  **Payment of Departmental Association Dues**:
    *   Pay your departmental student body dues (e.g., NACOS, ASES, IFELAW, SOSA, BAMSSA).
    *   Collect your **Official Departmental Handbook** (the definitive 4–5 year academic curriculum bible) and student ID badge.
3.  **HOD Formal Endorsement**: The Head of Department inspects the Course Advisor's signature and applies the official Departmental stamp.
4.  **Five-Way Course Form Distribution**:
    *   *Copy 1*: Personal student custody (preserve in your personal folder).
    *   *Copy 2*: Departmental Archive (submitted to Departmental Secretary).
    *   *Copy 3*: Faculty Officer (submitted to Faculty Office).
    *   *Copy 4*: Exams and Records Directorate (Senate Building).
    *   *Copy 5*: Division of Student Affairs (DSA).

## 📖 6. Hezekiah Oluwasanmi Library Registration

### Spatial Context & Reader Privileges
*   **Location**: Hezekiah Oluwasanmi Library (Central Academic Concourse).
*   **Office**: Readers' Services Division / Circulation Desk (Ground Floor).
*   **Operational Days**: Monday to Friday (8:00 AM – 10:00 PM during semesters); Saturday (8:00 AM – 4:00 PM).

### Prerequisite Document Dossier:
1.  **Fully Endorsed Course Registration Form**: Stamped by Course Advisor and HOD.
2.  **Stamped School Fees Remita Receipt**.
3.  **Faculty Clearance Certificate / Departmental Verification Slip**.
4.  **Two (2) Red-Background Passport Photographs**.

### Step-by-Step Library Pipeline:
1.  **Readers' Services Card Filling**: Fill out the physical library registration index card with your biographical details, department, matriculation number, and home/hostel address.
2.  **Issuance of Borrower's Pockets (Library Tickets)**:
    *   Freshmen receive **Borrowing Tickets/Cards**.
    *   Entitles the holder to borrow up to **two (2) to four (4) books** from the general open stacks for a loan duration of **14 calendar days**.
    *   Overdue book return incurs daily administrative fines.
3.  **E-Library & Digital Database Onboarding (First Floor)**:
    *   Register your institutional profile on the library network.
    *   Receive off-campus login access to international scholarly databases: **ScienceDirect, JSTOR, Research4Life, Scopus, and the OAU Institutional E-Repository**.
4.  **Library Tour & Cloakroom Rules**:
    *   **Cloakroom Mandatory Drop**: All personal backpacks, large bags, and jackets must be deposited at the Ground Floor cloakroom lockers before entry. Only notebooks, laptops, and transparent writing pouches are permitted inside.
    *   **Code of Absolute Silence**: Strict quiet policy enforced across all four floors; violators are ejected and have library cards suspended.

## 🛡️ 7. Freshman Clearance Survival & Anti-Scam Rules

1.  **Beware of "Clearance Fast-Track" Touts**: Unscrupulous individuals loiter around the Faculty and Health Centre complexes offering to *"help speed up your clearance file for ₦2,000 – ₦5,000"*. **Clearance is 100% free.** Never hand your original certificates to anyone outside an official office.
2.  **Prepare 20+ Passport Photographs in Advance**: Ensure you have a minimum of 20 identical passport photos featuring a **pure Red background**. OAU administrative units reject white, blue, or patterned backgrounds.
3.  **Keep a Dedicated Plastic File for Originals**: Keep your original WAEC, JAMB, and Birth Certificates inside an accordion plastic folder. Only present originals for visual sighting by officers; only hand over photocopies into the pale yellow file.
4.  **Keep Digital Backups**: Take clear CamScanner PDF scans of every single stamped document and upload them to your personal Google Drive or cloud storage. If a physical paper file goes missing, your digital timestamped copy proves completion.
